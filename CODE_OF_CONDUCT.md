# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be civil. Treat other players and contributors with respect, even in disagreement.

The 6b6t community includes people of all backgrounds. We do not tolerate harassment, hate speech, doxxing, spam, or targeting individuals or groups.

## Scope

This applies to this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to the 6b6t services it supports, including the Discord server and the website.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and ban accounts that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations on 6b6t services can lead to service bans.

Report violations to the maintainers on the 6b6t Discord.
